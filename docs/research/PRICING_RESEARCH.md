### What this implies for OddVex

- A serious research tier is expected at **$15–$30/mo**; above $30 the buyer expects either models or EV scanning. **[Assumption]** (synthesis)
- The cheapest credible anchors (Linemate $59.99/yr, Stat Sniper $7.99/mo) pull the floor down for beginner tools. **[Verified]** prices; **[Assumption]** on effect.
- EV/arb buyers tolerate $80–$500/mo, but they are a small, churn-prone, limit-prone segment, and feeding them requires low-latency data that costs far more (see `DATA_PROVIDER_EVALUATION.md`). **[Assumption]**
- AI usage has real marginal cost; every competitor meters it. OddVex should plan to meter too. **[Assumption]** on OddVex's own model costs; they are not estimated here.

## 3. Candidate OddVex tier structures

> **All three are proposals requiring Founder approval. No prices are final, no billing provider is to be configured, and no paywall is to be shipped on the basis of this document.** Price points are placeholders chosen to sit inside the verified competitor bands. Unit economics (data cost per user, AI cost per query, store fees) have **not** been modelled and must be before any decision.

### Proposal A — "Simple two-tier" (lowest risk, recommended starting point for discussion)

| | Free | OddVex Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Placeholder price | $0 | ~$14.99–$19.99/mo; ~$99–$119/yr |
| Odds comparison | Main markets, best price, freshness label | + alt lines, full book list |
| Props research | Limited props per day, basic hit rates | Full props analyzer, splits, matchup context |
| Bet tracker | Manual entry, basic P/L | + CLV, breakdowns, export |
| AI assistant | Small daily allowance | Larger monthly allowance |
| EV context | Fair-odds / no-vig display on a viewed market | Same, plus saved filters and alerts |
| Sportsbook sync | Not at launch | Not at launch (add later if the vendor cost is justified) |

- **For:** easy to explain; sits between Linemate and Outlier Premium; no dependency on expensive low-latency data.
- **Against:** no high-ARPU tier; AI allowance must be tuned carefully.

### Proposal B — "Three-tier ladder" (mirrors Outlier and BettingPros)

| | Free | Plus | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Placeholder price | $0 | ~$19.99/mo; ~$119/yr | ~$49.99–$79.99/mo; ~$299–$359/yr |
| Research + props | Limited | Full | Full |
| Tracker | Manual | CLV + analytics | + sync (if vendor approved) |
| AI assistant | Small allowance | Medium allowance | Large allowance |
| EV tools | Fair-odds display | EV indicators | Live EV feed, alerts, saved strategies |
| Backtesting / systems | — | View curated, documented systems | Build and backtest own filters |

- **For:** captures willingness to pay from sharper users; matches a pattern buyers already understand.
- **Against:** the Pro tier only works with a faster, costlier odds feed and (for sync) a ~$500+/mo vendor. Commits OddVex to competing on scanner speed, which is not where it can credibly win early.

### Proposal C — "Free core, pay for depth and AI" (Pikkit-style land grab)

| | Free | OddVex Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Placeholder price | $0 | ~$24.99–$29.99/mo; ~$149–$199/yr; optional season pass ~$59–$99 |
| Research + odds | Full for main markets and core props | + alt lines, deeper splits, history |
| Tracker | Full tracking | + CLV, exposure, scenario analysis |
| AI assistant | Small daily allowance | Large allowance, longer context |
| Monetisation extras | Clearly labelled sportsbook affiliate links (subject to legal review) | Same |

- **For:** fastest audience growth; season pass fits seasonal sports fans.
- **Against:** highest data and AI cost per non-paying user; affiliate revenue creates a conflict of interest with a transparency-first brand and brings state-level licensing questions. **[Assumption]** — needs legal advice, not researched here.

### Cross-cutting questions for the Founder

1. Is OddVex willing to carry free users' data and AI cost for growth (Proposal C), or should paid conversion come first (A/B)?
2. Is sportsbook-affiliate revenue acceptable for a product positioned on honesty? If yes, how is it disclosed?
3. Should the trial be card-free? Competitors mostly require a card; card-free is a trust signal but lowers conversion. **[Assumption]**
4. Web checkout vs in-app purchase: store fees and current store rules need a separate check before pricing is set. Not researched here.
5. What is the AI allowance unit (queries, tokens, "deep analyses")? Outlier's token model is the nearest reference. **[Verified · official]** https://www.outlier.bet/
6. Is an EV/arb tier in scope at all in year one? It drives the data-vendor decision more than anything else.
