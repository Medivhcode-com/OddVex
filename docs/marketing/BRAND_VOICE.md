# OddVex — Brand Voice

Last updated: 2026-11-15 · Applies to: product UI, marketing site, store listings, blog, email, social, support, assistant responses.

## 1. Brand in one paragraph

OddVex is a sports-betting intelligence platform: every line, compared and explained. We sound like a financial-analytics terminal that respects your time — exact numbers, clear labels, no noise — with the simplicity of a well-made consumer app. We inform; we never push. **Tagline: "Every Line. A Smarter Perspective."**

Boilerplate (use verbatim where a short description is needed):
> OddVex is a sports-betting intelligence platform from Medivh Code. Compare odds, research player props, run the maths and track your results in one place. OddVex does not accept or place wagers. 21+.

## 2. Personality → voice

| Trait | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Intelligent** | Explains the "why"; uses correct terms and defines them once | Showing off; jargon without definition |
| **Precise** | Exact numbers, units, timestamps, sample sizes | False precision; decimals beyond what the data supports |
| **Trustworthy** | States limits and sources; shows losses and uncertainty | Hedging everything into mush; legalese in UI |
| **Premium** | Calm, spare, confident; quality over volume | Luxury clichés; exclusivity; "VIP" |
| **Accessible** | Plain language first; expert detail on demand | Dumbing down; talking down |

## 3. Voice rules

1. **Lead with the number, then the meaning.** "−110 implies 52.4 %." 
2. **Describe, don't prescribe.** "Best available price: +105 at Book A" — not "Bet this at Book A".
3. **History is history.** "Over in 6 of the last 10 games" — not "trending over" or "due".
4. **Always attach time and source to data.** "As of 7:42 PM ET".
5. **State uncertainty plainly.** "Limited sample (4 games)".
6. **No urgency, no hype.** No exclamation marks in product UI; sparing elsewhere.
7. **Second person, active voice, short sentences.**
8. **Same weight for good and bad news.** A loss is "−$25.00", not a softened euphemism; a win is "+$22.73", not a celebration.
9. **Say what we are not.** Where confusion is possible: "OddVex does not accept or place wagers."
10. **Be honest about maturity.** Planned features are "planned"; demo data is "demo data"; the M1 assistant is "rule-based".

Tone by context:

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Data UI | Terse, neutral | "Stale — last updated 6 min ago" |
| Empty state | Helpful, one action | "No bets yet. Log your first bet to see your results." |
| Error | Calm, specific, actionable | "We couldn't load odds. Try again." |
| Education | Clear, patient | "Implied probability is the win chance a price represents, before removing the sportsbook's margin." |
| Marketing | Confident, concrete | "See every price side by side. Know what the number means." |
| Assistant | Direct, sourced, bounded | "Best moneyline price for Harbor City is +118 at Book C (as of 7:42 PM ET). I can't predict the result." |
| RG / sensitive | Warm, non-judgemental, direct | "If betting is causing you stress, free confidential help is available at 1-800-GAMBLER." |

## 4. Do / don't phrases

| Do say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Best available price" / "best line" | "Best bet", "top pick", "play of the day" |
| "Implied probability: 52.4 %" | "52 % chance to win" (as if it were our forecast) |
| "Over in 6 of the last 10" | "Hitting at 60 %", "smash the over", "can't miss" |
| "Expected value, based on your probability estimate" | "Profitable bet", "+EV lock", "free money" |
| "Compare prices across sportsbooks" | "Beat the books", "crush the sportsbooks" |
| "Track your results" | "Track your winnings" |
| "Research", "analyze", "compare", "understand" | "Win", "cash", "profit" (as promises) |
| "Informed decisions" | "Winning decisions", "smarter bets = more wins" |
| "May be correlated" | "Correlated for a bigger payout" |
| "Answers from verified data" | "AI predictions", "AI picks" |
| "Demo data" / "illustrative example" | Presenting fictional data as real |
| "Planned" / "in development" | "Coming soon!" with implied dates |
| "Sportsbook" | "Bookie" |
| "21+" | Any youth-oriented slang or memes |

Approved headline patterns:
- "Every Line. A Smarter Perspective."
- "See every price. Understand every number."
- "The clearest view of the betting market."
- "Your betting record, without the guesswork."
- "An assistant that shows its sources."

## 5. Banned claims (hard rules — no exceptions without counsel + Founder approval)

| # | Banned | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| BC-01 | Guaranteed or certain outcomes | "guaranteed", "lock", "sure thing", "can't lose", "100 %", "no-brainer" |
| BC-02 | Risk-free framing | "risk-free", "no risk", "free bet", "safe bet", "nothing to lose" |
| BC-03 | Income / wealth promises | "make money", "get rich", "quit your job", "side income", "pay your bills" |
| BC-04 | Unsubstantiated performance or accuracy | "X % accurate", "proven to win", "beats the market", "our users win more" |
| BC-05 | Fabricated social proof | Invented testimonials, user counts, ratings, press quotes, "as seen on" |
| BC-06 | Urgency / pressure to bet | "bet now", "don't miss out", "last chance", "before the line moves!" |
| BC-07 | Chasing / recovery | "win it back", "recover your losses", "bounce back" |
| BC-08 | Prediction claims for the assistant | "AI picks", "AI knows who wins", "predicts winners" |
| BC-09 | Implying OddVex takes bets or is affiliated with / endorsed by sportsbooks or leagues | "Bet with OddVex", "official odds partner" (unless contractually true and approved) |
| BC-10 | Skill-eliminates-risk claims | "take the luck out", "turn betting into investing" |
| BC-11 | Appeals to minors or to financial desperation | Cartoon mascots aimed at youth; "solve money problems" |
| BC-12 | Overstating data | "real-time" unless measured and true; "every sportsbook" unless literally true; "live" for delayed data |
| BC-13 | Overstating AI | Calling the rule-based M1 assistant an LLM or "advanced AI" |
| BC-14 | Legal assurances | "legal in your state", "100 % legal" — never without counsel-approved wording |

Note on the tagline: "Every Line" is brand language. In factual copy, do not claim coverage of every sportsbook or market unless verified (BC-12). Flag for counsel whether the tagline needs a qualifier in ads.

## 7. Terminology and style

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Product name | **OddVex** (capital O, capital V; never "Oddvex", "Odd Vex", "ODDVEX") |
| Assistant | **OddVex AI** (not "the bot", not a human name or persona) |
| Odds | American by default: +150, −110 (true minus sign where typography allows); decimal 2.50; probability one decimal: 52.4 % |
| Money | $1,250.00; negative as −$25.00; never colour alone |
| Time | 7:42 PM ET; relative ("6 min ago") only with absolute time available |
| Markets | moneyline, spread, total, player prop, parlay (lowercase) |
| Terms to define on first use | implied probability, vig/hold, no-vig, EV, CLV, correlation |
| Numbers | Never invent; never round to flatter; show sample sizes |
| Spelling | US English in product and marketing copy |
| Emoji | None in product UI; avoid in marketing |
| Capitalisation | Sentence case for headings and buttons |

## 8. Review checklist (every piece of copy)

- [ ] No banned claim (BC-01…BC-14)
- [ ] Every number is real, sourced, and timestamped — or clearly labelled illustrative
- [ ] No statement implies prediction, advice, or guaranteed outcome
- [ ] RG line present where required
- [ ] Feature availability stated accurately (available vs. planned)
- [ ] Reads clearly to a newcomer; precise enough for an expert
- [ ] External use approved by Founder (G-05)
