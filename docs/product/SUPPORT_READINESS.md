## 2. Ground rules for every reply

- OddVex does not take bets and cannot help with a bet placed at a sportsbook. Send the user to that sportsbook.
- Never give a pick, a prediction, or an opinion on whether a bet is good.
- Never call a number real while the data is synthetic.
- Never ask for, or accept, a password, a sportsbook login or a payment detail. If a user sends one, tell them to
  change it and delete it from the thread where the tool allows.
- Never promise a date, a feature or a price.
- If a message suggests gambling is causing harm, section 5.1 comes before anything else.
- Verify identity before discussing or changing an account (section 4.3). Without an admin tool, most account
  actions can only be done by the user in the app.
- Emailing a user is sending real customer email. Replying to a message they sent us through a published support
  address is the purpose of that address; anything proactive needs G-06.

## 3. Top expected questions

### 3.1 "Are these odds real?" / "I can't find this team or sportsbook"

**No.** Every team, player, sportsbook, price, score and statistic is fictional and generated by the software.
Nothing is licensed or live. The app says "Synthetic data" on screen and the API marks every response
`data_mode: "synthetic"`. One fictional sportsbook is always shown as stale on purpose, to demonstrate the label.

> The preview runs on synthetic data: the teams, players, sportsbooks and prices are fictional, and the app labels
> them that way. Nothing shown should be used to make a betting decision. Licensed data is planned; we don't have a
> date.

### 3.2 "Can I place a bet?" / "My bet at [sportsbook] wasn't paid"

OddVex never accepts, places or facilitates wagers and never moves money. The tracker is a record of bets placed
elsewhere. Settling a bet in the tracker changes only that record.

> OddVex is an information tool. It doesn't take bets or hold money, and it can't see or change a bet at a
> sportsbook. For anything about a bet you placed, contact that sportsbook directly.

### 3.3 "My alert didn't fire" / "It fired late" / "I didn't get a notification on my phone"

How it works:

- Alerts are checked **when you open the app** (specifically, when it reads your notifications or unread count,
  at most about every 30 seconds per user), when you press "Check now" on mobile, or when an operator runs a
  script. There is no background process.
- Notifications appear **inside the app only**. There is no push, email or text message.
- A price that reached your level and moved away again between two checks is not noticed. Prices are not stored.
- A "before the game starts" alert that was not checked before the game began expires without notifying.
- A rule tied to one game fires once, then shows as Triggered. "Set again" re-arms it. A team rule fires once per
  game.
- If a kind of notification is switched off in Settings, the rule still changes state but no notification appears.

Ask: which rule, its state now (Active, Triggered, Paused, Expired), and when the app was last opened.

> Alerts are checked when you open OddVex, not continuously, and they show up inside the app rather than as a push
> notification. If the price touched your level and moved back before the app was next opened, the alert won't have
> caught it. A start-time alert also needs the app to be opened before the game begins.

### 3.4 "I set a weekly limit and it still let me log a bet"

It is a **reminder, not a limit**, and the screens say so. OddVex never blocks, rejects or delays recording a bet.

- It counts only bets recorded in OddVex (entered or imported), not bets at sportsbooks it cannot see.
- The week runs Monday to Sunday in UTC, not the user's local time.
- It is checked when a bet is added or imported, not when one is edited, settled or deleted.
- One reminder a week at most, even if the amount is changed or the notification deleted.

> The weekly amount is a reminder that you set for yourself. OddVex can't stop a bet, because bets are placed at
> sportsbooks, not here, and it only counts bets you've recorded in the tracker. If you want a limit that is
> enforced, sportsbooks offer deposit and wager limits in their own settings.

Then consider section 5.1.

### 3.5 "How do I delete my account?" / "What happens to my data?"

- Web: Settings, Delete account, type DELETE. Mobile: Settings tab, Delete account, type DELETE.
- It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. There is no grace period.
- Removed: the account, bets and legs, watchlist, alert rules, notifications, notification preferences, the staking
  reminder, sync connections and import history.
- Also removed: stored account messages and all community content.
- **Kept:** security audit entries holding the account's internal id, the IP address and the detail of each past
  action (for example a bet's result or a followed team), with no email or name and no end date; daily database
  backups for about 14 days and the newest five pre-deploy backups, which still hold the account; server logs, which
  have no retention rule; and, if the account was suspended when deleted, its community handle, which stays reserved.
  Retention periods are not decided. Do not tell a user that "everything" is erased.
- If the user cannot sign in: the API can export or delete an account on its owner's behalf
  (`POST /v1/admin/users/{user_id}/export` and `/delete`, each needing a reason; delete also needs the account's email
  typed). **Do not use them yet.** No administrator exists on the preview, and who may run them, how the requester's
  identity is checked, how the export file is sent and destroyed, whether a deletion needs a second person and how
  long the reason text is kept are Founder decisions not yet written (section 6, item 10). Escalate under 5.5.

> You can delete your account in Settings > Delete account. It removes your account, bets, watchlist, alerts and
> notifications straight away and can't be undone. A security record of past actions, without your email or name,
> is kept, and backups still hold the account for about two weeks.

Check the wording of the last sentence with counsel before first use (`COUNSEL_REVIEW_PACKET.md` question 15).

### 3.6 "I forgot my password"

State at `2b0c7c1`, in production: **there is no way to recover a forgotten password.** The reset and
email-verification flows are built, but the `email_flows` switch is off in production, so "Forgot password?" is not
shown and no email is sent. The only password change is in Settings, and it needs the current password. Outside
production the flows write the email to an internal table and **send nothing**. Support must know which state is live
before answering.

After repeated failed sign-ins an email can also be refused for up to 15 minutes; with reset off, waiting is the only
way back in for the user (the operator's options are in `infrastructure/RUNBOOK.md`).

Today's honest answer:

> We can't reset a password yet. If you're still signed in on another device, you can change it under Settings >
> Change password using your current one. Otherwise the account can't be recovered at the moment.

Do not offer to change a password or email by hand. There is no tool and no safe way to verify the requester.

### 3.7 "I was signed out" / "It keeps asking me to log in"

- Sessions last 60 minutes by default and are not refreshed. After that the user signs in again.
- Changing the password signs out every other device by design.
- If everyone is signed out at once, operations may have rotated the signing secret (`infrastructure/RUNBOOK.md`
  section 7).

### 3.8 "Too many attempts. Wait a minute, then try again."

Sign-in, sign-up and password change are rate limited. The message means the limit was reached; waiting about a
minute clears it. If many users report it at once, escalate to engineering: web sign-ins currently share one limit
(`infrastructure/RUNBOOK.md` section 4.4).

### 3.9 "How do I download my data?"

Web: Settings, download your data. One JSON file with the profile, bets, watchlist, sync connections, import
history, alerts, notifications, preferences, the staking reminder and the user's own audit entries. It never
contains the password. **Not available in the mobile app**; use the web app.

### 3.10 "Is my sportsbook account connected?" / "Is it safe to connect?"

The connection in the preview is a demonstration. It imports sample bets from a fictional account, asks for no
username or password, and contacts no sportsbook. OddVex never asks for sportsbook credentials. If a sync fails,
pressing "Sync now" again is safe: it never duplicates bets.

### 3.11 "The profit or settlement on my bet is wrong"

- Ask whether the bet was settled by the user or "Settled from final score".
- Automatic settlement covers full-game moneyline, spread and total only, and only for bets tied to a game and a
  side. Props, futures, parlays with a pushed leg and their own price, and free-text bets are settled by hand.
- Scores are synthetic.
- If a calculation looks wrong, collect the bet's details and escalate as a data-integrity issue (section 5.3).
  Do not explain it away.

### 3.12 "What does OddVex AI do? It didn't understand me."

It is rule-based. It matches a small set of question types and answers from the app's own data with a sources
line. No language model is connected. It declines picks and guarantees. Fixed rules give crisis, under-21 and distress
messages a support reply with help resources instead of betting data, and every other answer ends with the helpline
and 988. The rules miss some wordings (`docs/compliance/RESPONSIBLE_GAMBLING.md` section 5): if a user reports that the
assistant answered a message about distress or self-harm with betting data, treat it as 5.1 and tell engineering.

### 3.13 "How much does it cost?" / "When is Premium?"

It is free. No price has been set and nothing can be bought. Do not speculate.

### 3.14 "Where is the mobile app?"

Not in any store. The download page says the apps are in development. One Android test build exists for the Founder's own phone; it is signed with a public debug key and must not be shared (`apps/mobile/README.md`, "Builds"). The app has not been reported as tested on devices.

### 3.15 "Is this legal in my state?" / "Can I use it if I'm 19?"

OddVex is for adults 21 and over everywhere. Do not give legal advice or a state-by-state answer; none has been
approved by counsel. If someone says they are under 21, section 5.4.

### 3.16 "Which numbers can I trust? How is this calculated?"

Point to `/methodology`. Past hit rates are a record, not a forecast, and are never a probability of a future
result.

```
Thanks for asking. The OddVex preview runs on synthetic data: the teams, players,
sportsbooks and prices are fictional and labelled that way in the app. Nothing shown
should be used to make a betting decision. Licensed data is planned and we don't
have a date yet.
```

### 4.2 Bet placed elsewhere

```
OddVex doesn't take bets or hold money, so we can't see or change a bet at a
sportsbook. For anything about that bet, please contact [sportsbook] directly.
The tracker in OddVex is your own record, and you can edit or delete entries there.
```

### 4.3 Identity check before discussing an account

```
To keep your account safe we only discuss account details with its owner. Please
reply from the email address on the account. We will never ask for your password
or for any sportsbook login.
```

### 4.4 Account deletion

```
You can delete your account yourself in Settings > Delete account (type DELETE to
confirm). It removes your account, bets, watchlist, alerts and notifications
immediately and can't be undone. [Retained-data sentence approved by counsel.]
If you'd like a copy first, use Settings > download your data in the web app.
```

### 4.5 Alerts

```
Alerts in OddVex are checked when you open the app, not continuously, and they
appear inside the app rather than as push notifications. If a price reached your
level and moved back before the app was next opened, the alert won't have caught
it. You can see each alert's state under [Alerts], and set a triggered one again.
```

### 4.6 Staking reminder

```
The weekly amount is a reminder you set for yourself, not a limit. OddVex can't
block a bet, and it only counts bets recorded in your tracker. Limits that are
enforced are available in each sportsbook's own settings.
```

### 4.7 Forgotten password (state on main)

```
We're not able to reset passwords yet. If you're still signed in on another
device, you can set a new one under Settings > Change password with your current
password. Otherwise the account can't be recovered right now. We're sorry for
the trouble.
```

### 4.8 Responsible gambling

```
Thank you for telling us. You don't have to handle this alone. The National
Problem Gambling Helpline is confidential and answers 24 hours a day, every day:
call 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738) or text 800GAM. It is not an emergency
service. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text
988 now, or call 911 if you are in immediate danger.

If it would help, you can delete your OddVex account in Settings > Delete account.
We won't send you anything about betting.
```

Helpline details were last checked on 2026-11-19 against the National Council on Problem Gambling's pages and must be re-verified before each release (`docs/compliance/RESPONSIBLE_GAMBLING.md`). Never give 1-800-GAMBLER as the national helpline: it is the Council on Compulsive Gambling of New Jersey's line. Do not add
betting information to this reply.

### 4.9 Feature or date request

```
Thanks for the suggestion. We don't share dates for things that aren't built,
so we can't promise this, but we've noted it.
```

### 4.10 Security report

```
Thank you for reporting this. We've passed it to the team responsible and will
reply by [date]. Please don't share the details publicly while we look into it,
and don't send passwords or other secrets by email.
```
